Full Text

Welcome to this explainer. Today we're asking a big question. Why are Japanese workplaces so unhappy? Let me ask you, do you really like your job? Are you actually happy at work? Well, in Japan, that number is super low. Only 5% of workers feel excited today. That 5% is way, way lower than the global average of 23%. Compared to places like India, Japan actually ranks dead last for job happiness.

So why are workers there so sad? What exactly causes this huge office problem? The reasons are simple. Super long hours, strict bosses, no holidays, and zero chance to share ideas. There's also shushin coyo or lifetime employment. This old rule makes people feel totally stuck. Because they feel stuck, only 25% think it's a good time to find a new job.

Okay, let's dive in. What happens when people feel this unhappy? Let's look at the bad results. A huge chunk, 32% of workers feel high stress almost every single day. Happy workers learn new things, but sad workers just do the bare minimum. 73% of workers just show up. They bring zero energy or passion to the office. With that low energy, workers stop growing.

So, how can we fix it? How can companies make things better? First, listen to workers by holding open meetings. Next, make friendly rules and build real friendships. Bosses must include everyone. For example, they can even pay for outside classes to help workers learn. Fixing this helps everyone. Happy workers build a much stronger, faster, and more productive company.
